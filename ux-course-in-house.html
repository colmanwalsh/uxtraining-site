In-house courses

UX Foundation

Everything your team needs to know about UX. From the fundamental concepts to detailed design and research techniques. Our most popular course with corporate clients looking to change mindsets and sharpen skills. Read more about this course.
Duration: 2 days

Mobile UX

Detailed look at specific mobile UX design principles. Includes best practice considerations for navigation, workflows, micro-tasks and iOS v Android. Read more about this course.

Why work with us

International experience

You'll benefit from 20+ years experience of commercial UX projects in San Francisco, New York, London, Dublin and across the EU.

Industry experience

First-hand knowledge of major industries including telcos, financial services, retail, property, government, utilities, and pure-play online services.

Process focus

We believe in process. Our course explains clearly where UX sits within both Agile and Waterfall software development processes

Practical focus

We’re not theoretical. We want your team to come away from our courses with knowledge and techniques they can apply back in the office the next day. 

Learn-by-doing

We don't deliver lectures. We believe in learn-by-doing. Your team will be on their feet, working in teams, getting hands-on practical experience
