What? Why? Who?

What is the Mobile UX course?

A fast-paced one-day workshop where you’ll learn the fundamentals of designing high-quality mobile interfaces. Using a real-world case-study and lots of practical examples, we’ll show you how to use the essential building blocks to design your own mobile app.

Why should I attend?

Mobile is everywhere. If you’re working in technology, you need to understand the basic concepts of what makes a good (and bad) mobile product. You won’t be listening to a boring lecture, you’ll be redesigning a mobile app in a team environment. You’ll leave the course with confidence, know-how and practical techniques you can use back at work.

Who should attend?

Anybody involved in designing, building or managing mobile websites or applications. You might be a UX designer with a mobile-sized gap in your CV. Or a product manager who wants to understand mobile best practice. Or maybe you just want to keep your skills up-to-date in a mobile-first world. If any of this sounds familiar, you’ll benefit from this course.

Course outline

A one-day course where you’ll redesign a real mobile application.

module 1

High-level design

The mobile landscape

  • Introducing our case study
  • What is the problem it’s trying to solve?
  • Why solve it on mobile?
  • Responsive or native?

High-level design

  • Understanding the different application types
  • Designing the high-level flow
  • Choosing a navigation model
  • Navigation elements
module 2

Workflows

On-boarding

  • Sign-up and registration
  • Login and password retrieval
  • Taking payments
  • Using payment gateways 

Common workflows

  • Displaying and choosing content
  • Complex processes and forms
  • Micro-tasks
module 3

Mobile details

Saving time

  • Error handling and prevention
  • Minimising inputs
  • Smart defaults
  • Smart selectors

Communications

  • What does this app do?
  • Notifications
  • In-app messages
module 4

Putting it all together

Putting it all together

  • Wireframes
  • Wireflows
  • Team presentations
